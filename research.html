CosmoBench-Gen

I generate cosmology problems with known solutions, then introduce sign errors, unit mistakes and missing terms. The grader classified all 12 synthetic validation responses correctly.

Scientific machine learning

CosmoEmulator-NN

Using 48,000 samples from 1,200 simulated cosmologies, I built a neural surrogate for distance modulus. Its held-out RMS residual was 14.3 mmag. In this benchmark, it ran about 35× faster than numerical integration.

PINN-CosmoSolver

I trained a neural network against the cosmological evolution equation and compared it with an independent RK45 solution. The largest relative difference in the validation run was 0.004%.

DarkEnergy-ModelClassifier

Could noisy Hubble diagrams distinguish three dark-energy models? A principal-component and neural-network classifier reached 74.2% accuracy on simulated test data. Random guessing would yield about 33%.
